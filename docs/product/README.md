# Product

## Vision

PartnerHub exists to give entrepreneurs in MLM, direct selling, affiliates, distributors, and commercial networks one platform for web assets, validated messages, acquisition channels, traffic context, and external lead routing.

PartnerHub is not a CRM. It does not manage leads after they are routed to the entrepreneur's external commercial channel.

## Business Model

The product is designed for recurring revenue through SaaS subscriptions, enterprise plans, setup services, and premium add-ons. Pricing can be shaped by tenant size, feature tier, usage volume, and support needs.

## PRD Direction

The initial PRD should focus on the smallest useful platform that can serve one tenant well while preserving the ability to scale to many tenants later.

Priority areas:

- tenant setup
- user and entrepreneur identity
- role-based permissions
- core administrative controls
- web asset packages
- master assets and validated messages
- personalized channels
- external lead destinations
- traffic generation context
- reporting visibility without post-routing lead management

## Product Principles

- Support real asset, message, publishing, and routing work, not just dashboards.
- Keep the first release narrow and reliable.
- Make entrepreneur acquisition assets understandable.
- Design for expansion into future industries without redesign.
