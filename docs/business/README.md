# Business

## Pricing

PartnerHub should support flexible pricing so it can serve small teams, growing networks, and enterprise tenants.

Potential pricing dimensions:

- number of companies or tenants
- active users
- partner count
- module access
- transaction or volume tiers
- support level

## Commercial Strategy

The product should be positioned as a platform that helps organizations manage distributed growth more efficiently than spreadsheets, disconnected tools, or custom one-off systems.

## Growth

Long-term growth should come from:

- strong tenant retention
- modular expansion
- integrations
- white-label readiness
- operational reliability

## Market Fit

The platform is especially relevant for organizations with:

- structured partner hierarchies
- commissions or incentives
- recurring onboarding needs
- multi-region operations
- a need for auditability and centralized control
