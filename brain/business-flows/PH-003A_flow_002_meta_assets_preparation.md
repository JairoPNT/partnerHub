# PH-003A - Flow 002: Meta Assets Preparation

## 1. Purpose

Document the complementary Meta assets flow for preparing Facebook, Instagram, Business Portfolio / Business Manager, ad account, payment method, alerts, and campaign readiness.

Meta Setup supports launch and ads readiness. It does not replace Flow 001 and does not block initial landing publication unless the contracted service requires social launch or ads.

## 2. Scope

Included:

- Guided Meta setup preparation.
- Existing asset validation.
- Facebook Page readiness.
- Instagram Business/Creator readiness.
- Business Portfolio / Business Manager guidance when applicable.
- Ad account and payment method checks.
- Manual tasks and alerts.
- Campaign readiness checks.

Excluded:

- Automatic promise of Facebook or Instagram account creation.
- Automatic creation of personal profiles.
- Automatic mass account creation.
- Simulated human activity.
- Attempts to evade Meta verification, restrictions, or policy reviews.

## 3. What PartnerHub can do

PartnerHub can:

- Guide the business owner to configure or connect a Facebook Page.
- Guide the business owner to configure or connect Instagram Business/Creator.
- Guide Business Portfolio / Business Manager setup when applicable.
- Guide ad account setup.
- Guide payment method setup.
- Validate evidence or connection status.
- Generate alerts and manual tasks.
- Prepare campaign materials when the service is contracted.

## 4. What PartnerHub cannot do

PartnerHub cannot:

- Promise automatic creation of Facebook accounts.
- Promise automatic creation of Instagram accounts.
- Create Facebook personal profiles.
- Automatically create personal Instagram accounts.
- Automate mass account creation.
- Simulate human activity.
- Evade Meta verifications.
- Publish without valid permissions.
- Run ads from restricted or incomplete assets.
- Publish medical, guaranteed income, or guaranteed results claims without validation.

## 5. Entry point

The flow may start after:

- Business owner purchase flow starts.
- Payment is confirmed.
- Onboarding checklist starts.
- A contracted service requires social launch or ads.
- An admin manually requests Meta preparation.

## 6. Main flow

1. Identify whether Meta Setup is required by the contracted service.
2. Ask whether the business owner already has a Facebook Page.
3. Ask whether the business owner already has Instagram.
4. Validate whether Instagram is Business or Creator.
5. Ask whether Instagram is connected to the Facebook Page.
6. Ask whether Business Portfolio / Business Manager exists when applicable.
7. Ask whether an ad account exists when campaigns are contracted.
8. Ask whether a payment method exists when campaigns are contracted.
9. Generate alerts for missing or blocked assets.
10. Generate manual instructions for the business owner.
11. Prepare campaign readiness only when campaigns are contracted.
12. Trigger manual review when restrictions, sensitive claims, or new-account risks exist.

## 7. Cascading onboarding instructions

Cascading order:

1. Facebook Page.
2. Instagram Business/Creator.
3. Instagram to Facebook Page connection.
4. Business Portfolio / Business Manager if applicable.
5. Ad account if campaigns are contracted.
6. Payment method if campaigns are contracted.
7. Permissions and restrictions review.
8. Campaign readiness review.

Each step can produce:

- Complete.
- Pending.
- Manual action required.
- Blocked.
- Waiting period recommended.
- Compliance review required.

## 8. Outputs

Outputs may include:

- Meta setup checklist.
- Facebook Page status.
- Instagram status.
- Business Portfolio / Business Manager status.
- Ad account status.
- Payment method status.
- Manual onboarding instructions.
- Alerts.
- Campaign readiness result.
- Manual review tasks.

## 9. Alerts

Supported alert severities:

- `INFO`
- `WARNING`
- `BLOCKER`
- `WAIT`
- `COMPLIANCE`

Alert rules:

- Missing setup that does not block the landing may be `INFO`.
- Missing items that affect launch readiness may be `WARNING`.
- Missing permissions, restricted accounts, missing payment methods, or missing ad accounts may be `BLOCKER`.
- New accounts that may require time or review before advertising may be `WAIT`.
- Sensitive claims or policy risks must be `COMPLIANCE`.

## 10. Campaign readiness

Campaigns are an additional service.

Campaign readiness requires:

- Contracted campaign service.
- Facebook Page ready.
- Instagram Business/Creator ready when required.
- Business Portfolio / Business Manager ready when required.
- Ad account ready.
- Payment method ready.
- Budget approved.
- Administration cost approved.
- No unresolved Meta restrictions.
- Sensitive claims manually validated.

New accounts may require waiting or review before ads can run.

## 11. Manual review triggers

Manual review is required when:

- Meta restrictions exist.
- Permissions are missing or expired.
- Page Publishing Authorization is pending.
- Ad account is missing or restricted.
- Payment method is missing.
- Business Portfolio / Business Manager ownership is unclear.
- The account is new and campaign risk is high.
- Medical, income guarantee, results guarantee, or other sensitive claims appear.

## 12. Open questions

Open questions:

- Will PartnerHub use Meta Login from MVP or manual checklist?
- Will social posts be manual publishing packs or API from a future phase?
- Who owns the Business Portfolio?
- Are campaigns launched from the business owner's account or a managed account?
- What is the ad administration cost?
- What is the recommended minimum ad budget?
- Which Gano Excel claims are allowed?
- Who approves pieces with health, income, or results claims?
