# PH-007C Manual Referral Benefit

Status: Approved product rule; manual operation

## Objective

Provide a PartnerHub service benefit for qualified referrals without creating an automatic multi-level compensation system.

## Approved rule

- Every two qualified PartnerHub referrals generate one monthly support credit.
- Credits accumulate without a technical ceiling.
- Twelve months is a visible milestone, not a maximum balance.
- The dashboard may show `12/12` and then continue with a message such as `Meta superada: 15 meses disponibles`.
- Credits are applied to future PartnerHub support invoices and cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred, or used to pay installation fees.

## Qualified referral

A referral becomes qualified only after:

1. The new entrepreneur enters the referring entrepreneur's approved code.
2. An operator validates the relationship.
3. The referred entrepreneur pays the installation fee.
4. The page is generated and published.
5. The referred entrepreneur pays the first support period and remains active without refund or cancellation.

The operator controls the final qualification in the manual MVP. No credit is created merely for entering a code or registering a person.

## Credit application

- One available credit covers one support billing cycle at the beneficiary's active plan price.
- Under the approved 2026-08-03 MVP pricing model, this means one credit covers $40.000 COP for a beneficiary with 1 ecosystem or $60.000 COP for a beneficiary with 2 ecosystems / all-in-one.
- Credits are applied on the next due date and recorded in an immutable ledger.
- A plan change affects the value of future applied credits, not the number of credits already earned.
- If a referred customer cancels, refunds, or becomes invalid before qualification, the referral does not generate a credit.
- The benefit is a commercial PartnerHub incentive and must remain separate from GanoExcel's own compensation plan.
- Credits cannot pay activations, annual renewal/reactivation, advertising campaigns, ad spend, personalization, video production, AI generation, or other extras.

## Manual dashboard data

- Referring entrepreneur and approved external code.
- Referred entrepreneur and site ID.
- Relationship status: pending, validated, qualified, rejected, cancelled.
- Qualification date and operator.
- Qualified referral count.
- Credits earned, applied, reversed, and available.
- Ledger reason and related customer/payment reference.

## Future automation gate

Automation is deferred until payment, refund, subscription status, permissions, audit, and legal review are stable. The first implementation should expose the ledger and operator actions without automatically applying discounts.
