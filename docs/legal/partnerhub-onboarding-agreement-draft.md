# PartnerHub Onboarding Agreement: Technical Draft

This is a drafting aid for legal review. It is not a signed contract and must not be presented as final legal advice.

## Commercial service

The entrepreneur contracts the creation and operation of a static product page under the selected PartnerHub plan. The agreement must identify setup price, recurring support price, payment due date, what support includes, what is outside scope, suspension for non-payment, cancellation, and renewal.

The one-time installation fee is paid in full before production begins. After payment and receipt of the required information and assets, the target installation window is 24 to 48 hours. Recurring support is paid monthly in advance, with the due date falling on the same calendar day on which the service starts each month. PartnerHub may send a reminder three days before the due date, another on the due date, and a further notice on the third day of arrears. A five-calendar-day grace period applies after the support due date; after that period, the service may be suspended. Reactivation requires receipt of the outstanding payment. Suspension of support does not automatically release the domain; domain expiry and release are handled separately under the final agreement.

The service is an administration and optimization service. Template changes may be made after reviewing performance and proposed improvements. No specific number of leads, sales, conversion rate, or income is guaranteed.

## Optional PartnerHub referral benefit

If the selected offer includes the PartnerHub referral benefit, each two qualified referrals may generate one monthly support credit. A qualified referral requires operator validation, payment of the referred entrepreneur's installation fee, publication of the referred page, and payment of the first support period without refund or cancellation. Credits accumulate without a technical ceiling; twelve months is an indicative milestone and not a cap. Credits apply only to future PartnerHub support periods, are not exchangeable for cash, are not transferable, and do not pay installation fees. The benefit is separate from any compensation plan or commercial relationship of GanoExcel. The final agreement must state the operator's validation process and reversal conditions.

## Control and ownership

The domain, hosting account, VPS, R2 storage, PartnerHub environment, source template, deployment tooling, and shared system remain administered by Jairo Pinto while the service is active.

The entrepreneur retains rights in original materials they provide, subject to the license required to operate the contracted page. Gano Excel product images, trademarks, videos, and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights and are not transferred as entrepreneur-owned assets.

If the entrepreneur requests technical control, the parties may document a handoff. The handoff ends PartnerHub service for that page. The entrepreneur then assumes full responsibility for domain, hosting, files, deployments, security, maintenance, and updates. Later custom work by Jairo requires a new service agreement.

The handoff package may include the entrepreneur's own supplied assets and a copy of the generated static package. It must not promise transfer of third-party assets or PartnerHub infrastructure.

## Domain lifecycle

The domain is not transferred automatically. If the service is not renewed, the agreement should define advance notice, a final renewal window, what is disabled at expiry, and that eventual public availability is controlled by the registrar's grace and redemption periods.

## Improvements

Entrepreneurs may submit improvement proposals. PartnerHub may evaluate them with the project team and, where appropriate, organizational leaders. An adopted improvement may be incorporated into the shared template and replicated across pages. Submission does not guarantee adoption, exclusivity, payment, or individual implementation unless separately agreed in writing.

## Data and media permissions

The agreement should contain separate, purpose-specific checkboxes for service acceptance, personal-data processing, page publication of supplied media, and optional portfolio use. The text must identify the responsible party, purposes, data rights, contact channel, retention, and relevant processors.

## Evidence

PartnerHub should create a versioned snapshot and restricted PDF after acceptance, with typed signature, optional drawn signature, server timestamps, IP/user agent, and hash. Access to these records must be restricted and retention must be defined in the privacy policy.
