# Product Model

## Product Vision

PartnerHub is a SaaS platform for web assets, validated messages, and acquisition channels for direct selling, network marketing, distributors, affiliates, MLM entrepreneurs, and commercial partner ecosystems.

PartnerHub is not a CRM. It attracts, educates, and routes interested people toward external commercial channels controlled by the entrepreneur.

## First Implementation

- First tenant / launch implementation: Gano Excel
- Product design must still support future tenants without structural redesign

## Product Principles

- Solve real asset creation, message validation, publishing, and routing work.
- Keep the first release narrow and reliable.
- Make entrepreneur acquisition assets understandable.
- Preserve room for white-label and multi-tenant growth.

## Core Product Areas

- entrepreneur onboarding
- identity and access
- web asset packages
- master assets and validated messages
- personalized channels
- lead destinations controlled by the entrepreneur
- traffic generation context
- reporting and visibility
- administration and tenancy controls

## Future Expansion

- advanced landing builder
- advanced VSL builder
- creative assets
- traffic campaign manager
- AI Content Studio research and implementation
- automations

## Publishing Model

Generated public websites, landing pages, VSL pages, and campaign pages should be treated as static or lightweight published artifacts whenever possible.

The product should control creation, validation, publication, routing, and traceability, but the web hosting layer should serve the public output to reduce VPS processing cost.

The PartnerHub visitor flow ends when the visitor is routed to the entrepreneur's external LeadDestination, such as WhatsApp, checkout, external form, booking link, social DM, phone, or another approved external channel.

## AI Content Studio

Future EPIC-800 should research HeyGen and ElevenLabs integrations for automated VSL and audiovisual content generation with:

- consent controls
- review before publication
- versioning
- audit logging
- cost visibility

The broader platform should also track the operational costs of:

- HeyGen video generation
- ElevenLabs audio generation
- storage
- n8n executions when applicable
- domain and hosting costs
- future Meta Ads spend
